How we work together. Live site

1:1 COACHING — SUB-SECTION C: FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might be wondering. Live site

What exactly does a burnout coach do? Live site
A burnout coach helps you get to the root of your burnout, step out of harmful cycles, and build systems to support a healthier and more sustainable life and career.
How is burnout coaching different from therapy or career coaching? Live site
This work lives in the middle of the Venn diagram between your health, habits, and work. At Resilient Resonance, we combine trauma-informed coaching, nervous system regulation techniques, and design and innovation strategies to help you recover, recalibrate, and rebuild.
I'm not sure how burned out I am. Is this still for me? Live site
Of course. You don't have to hit rock bottom to benefit from support. If your stress is starting to affect your health or performance, working with a burnout coach can help you figure out what needs to change.
What kinds of outcomes can I expect? Live site
Every client's path is different. But common outcomes include reduced stress, better energy management, clearer boundaries, improved job performance, and more clarity around your next steps — which could be a role or career change, a sabbatical, or something else.
What if I'm not sure this is the right kind of support? Live site
Start with a free, no-pressure intro call. You'll learn more about how we work and get to ask any questions you have.
